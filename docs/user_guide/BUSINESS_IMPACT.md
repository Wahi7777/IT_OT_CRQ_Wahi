# Business Impact (sheet 04)

- Operational percentiles are annual aggregates (most years zero), not financial VaR.
- **Financial loss categories** are Balbix major cost categories rolled up from applicable drivers only.
- **Applicable impact drivers** list enabled, scenario-applicable drivers with AAL and portfolio TVaR 99 contributions.
- Monetary theft / funds at risk appear under **Direct Loss of Funds**, never under Incident & response.
- OT assessments may show **Balbix catalogue gaps** for consequences (safety, environmental, major equipment) that have no Balbix driver ID yet — these are flagged, not invented.
